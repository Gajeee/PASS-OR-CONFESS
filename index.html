18+ WARNING

This mode has mature questions, dares, and a drinking mechanic. Drink responsibly, know your limits, and only play if everyone at the table is 18 or older.

How To Play

1. The Setup

Add players, set the timer, and pick a difficulty. Or flip on 18+ Drinking Mode from the home screen.

2. Your Turn

Read the card. It's either a Truth or a Dare. You have two choices.

Confess:

On a Truth, answer honestly — the group rates it 1-5 for how bold it was, that's your points. On a Dare, just do it.

Pass:

Skip the card, lose 1 point, and take a random penalty.

18+ Mode:

Score doesn't matter here — a low rating on the Confession Meter means you drink, and Passing is always a shot.
